Online scores & migration

Your nickname, score and run details are public when you save online. Use a nickname, not private information. This is a casual leaderboard: runs are reported by players, not independently verified.

Your device scores stay yours

Existing browser records are never deleted by migration. Eligible records automatically sync to the Online leaderboard. Older scores may lack timing information; unknown fields remain unknown. Unreadable records remain untouched. Pending submissions retry automatically without creating another copy of the same run.

Limited private reporting

Online mode uses Firebase anonymous sign-in and a randomly generated installation identifier. The game owner can see whether that installation had old scores, imported them, encountered failures or duplicates, and used the online leaderboard. These identifiers do not identify a person across devices. Clearing browser data can create a new identifier.

No Google Analytics, fingerprinting, advertising IDs, location, session-history tracking or device profiling is enabled by this score feature. Firebase processes service requests under its own privacy information; this is not a promise of no provider connection logs.

Your choices

The This device tab shows this browser’s scores; Online combines players’ imported and new scores. Viewing This device does not pause automatic online synchronization. Previously shared scores remain online until the owner removes them. A request already in flight may complete. Closing the app pauses retries; reopening can resume them.

Scores are retained until removed by the owner. Private migration diagnostics are reviewed for removal after90days; no automatic deletion service is configured. Ask the game owner about removing a score or related private records; do not post private identifiers in public issues. Local clear only clears this browser's leaderboard and cannot clear everyone else's online scores.

Offline installation is not included. While a loaded game loses connection, device scores and a persistent submission queue can still work where browser storage is available. Storage clearing/eviction or private browsing can remove unsynced records.
