# Asset and dependency provenance

Level geometry, skyline, drawn NPCs, props, typography layout, jokes, and Web Audio effects are original code in this repository. Seth’s character and walking sheet, Allison, and both cats were made using the built-in image-generation tool from Nate’s owner-supplied photographs and corrections; the comedy clown, scruffy pedestrian, fictional soccer star and happy concert fans were generated from original prompts. Raw reference photographs are not included. Thirteen WAV recordings were supplied by Nate from Downloads on October 26 and copied unchanged under public/audio/ for use in this game. No voice synthesis/cloning or external voice service is used. Browser TTS remains disabled. The Minnesota United crest (`public/assets/minnesota-united-user-supplied.png`) was provided directly by Nate on October 26 for the fan scene. It remains third-party team artwork/trademark; no license or endorsement is claimed. Display uses the original pixels and a texture crop. No third-party stock art, downloaded audio/fonts, starter-game code, or venue assets. Browser/system Arial/Helvetica sans-serif is used. The intro skyline (`public/assets/intro-skyline.png`) was generated with the built-in image tool from Nate’s supplied city photo; the raw photo is not included. Original sound effects are synthesized locally; no downloaded audio is included.

Runtime dependency: Phaser 3.90.0 (MIT), Copyright (c) 2013-2025 Phaser Studio Inc. Reuses its rendering, input, camera and Arcade Physics systems. Its full MIT notice is retained in `licenses/phaser-MIT.txt` and installed package LICENSE.md. Source: https://github.com/phaserjs/phaser/tree/v3.90.0

Build tools: Vite 8.3.5 (MIT), TypeScript 7.0.2 (Apache-2.0). These are development dependencies; their license texts and transitive dependency notices remain in installed packages. Lockfile pins exact resolution. Phaser transitively uses eventemitter3 (MIT). Retained full notice in `licenses/eventemitter3-MIT.txt`.

Original source/art: project-owned; no additional redistribution license is granted. Third-party rights remain with their respective owners.

The title logo and five additional WAV recordings (elevator, goal, crashing, quick jump, cheater) were supplied by the project owner October 26, 2026. The quiet final tower and impact debris are original code-drawn artwork.

The realistic six-pack pickup (public/assets/beer-pickup.png) was generated with the built-in image-generation tool from an original prompt: six amber bottles in a tan cardboard carrier, transparent background, no commercial branding. The Play Now graphic is original SVG artwork.
